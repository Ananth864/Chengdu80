# Headlines and Supporting Analysis

## When to Use

Use for asset identity, the one-year performance chart, industry trends, headlines and supporting research preparation. Follow [agent.md](../agent.md). These components do not add financial metrics to either six-metric menu.

Research preparation is separate from chatbot answering. The answering agent may use only accepted prepared material and cannot start external research during an answer.

## Industry and Company Context

- Prepare sourced context about demand, supply/capacity, pricing, costs, policy, seasonality, technology and relevant market conditions.
- Include material rate, currency and commodity exposures only when supported.
- Retain supported business model, products, revenue sources, segment details, acquisitions and unusual items.
- Attribute management explanations rather than treating them as established causes.
- Limit governance, concentration, auditor, legal, environmental and commitment claims to disclosed/prepared evidence.

## Headlines

1. Match a story using the company's unique identity and verified aliases or its validated industry. A shared keyword alone is insufficient.
2. Deduplicate related stories and revisions without losing source provenance. Distinguish a new event from a republication of an older event.
3. Retain headline, source link/reference, publication date, event date when known, matched identity/industry, and supporting excerpts or accepted facts.
4. Separate verified events from possible implications. Do not invent news or imply causation between a story and a price move without evidence.
5. Apply the approved headline window and freshness policy. These remain undecided; do not invent them or label an unchecked story current.
6. Refresh prepared analysis when accepted events or inputs change; exclude superseded interpretations from the current snapshot.

## One-Year Performance History

- Retain one year of dated observations for chart and trend support, with asset identity, source, units/currency and accepted observation dates.
- Label the chart's actual period and whether it shows prices or a defined return measure. Price change is not automatically total return.
- Corporate-action adjustment, dividend reinvestment, trading-calendar and missing-observation conventions require validated definitions; do not silently choose them.
- Do not fabricate observations, fill missing records with zero or use an old endpoint as a current quote.
- Short available history must remain visibly short. Do not imply a long stable dividend or performance record from one year of observations.
