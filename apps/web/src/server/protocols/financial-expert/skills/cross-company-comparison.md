# Cross-Company Comparison

## When to Use

Use only for the explicitly requested comparison of two or more companies. This separate feature permits naming those selected companies; it does not turn the ordinary industry-benchmark dashboard into a competitor dashboard. Follow [agent.md](../agent.md).

## Approved Measures

| Measure                            | Proposed calculation/display convention                                                           | Required inputs                                                                  |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Revenue YoY growth                 | (current annual revenue - prior annual revenue) / prior annual revenue * 100, in %                | Comparable annual revenue with positive prior base                               |
| Gross profit margin change         | Current annual gross margin - prior annual gross margin, in percentage points                     | Two definition-matched margins                                                   |
| ROE change                         | Current reported weighted-average ROE - prior reported weighted-average ROE, in percentage points | Same weighted-average method and profit scope in both years and across companies |
| Liabilities-to-assets ratio change | Current year-end ratio - prior year-end ratio, in percentage points                               | Total liabilities/total assets * 100 at both year ends                           |

The four measures are confirmed; percentage-point presentation and revision alignment are proposed conventions to approve before release. A change from 10% to 12% is a 2-percentage-point increase, not a 2% relative increase. Do not silently substitute relative percentage changes for differences in ratios.

## Procedure

1. Resolve each company to unique code and exchange; preserve leading zeros and ask for disambiguation where needed.
2. Confirm requested fiscal years and annual-report basis. Display exact reporting windows. Do not present materially different fiscal windows as equivalent; ask for alignment or explain that comparison is unavailable.
3. Validate current/prior fields, currency/scale, consolidation scope, release dates, revisions and metric definitions for each company.
4. Use restated prior comparatives only under an approved revision policy, with consistent within-company bases and visible restatement notes. Do not mix original and revised values silently.
5. For ROE use the added annual-report dashboard's weighted-average method, not the original investment dashboard's TTM/simple-average-equity proposal. Incompatible provider methods prevent an ordinary like-for-like comparison.
6. Request the four validated calculations, then present a company-by-measure table with periods, units, evidence and qualifications.
7. Preserve valid cells when another company's measure is unavailable. Explain gaps; do not substitute unapproved metrics or rank missing values as zero.

## Limits and Interpretation

- Nonpositive prior revenue prevents normal percentage-growth interpretation. State the limitation rather than generating a misleading rate.
- Gross margin requires a suitable business model and matching revenue/cost scope. Invalid equity limits ordinary ROE interpretation. Assets must be positive for liabilities-to-assets.
- Higher sales growth or improved margin/ROE can be described as stronger on that measure only with comparability and leverage/base qualifications.
- A liabilities-to-assets increase means liabilities rose relative to assets, not proof of imminent financial distress. A decrease is not automatically good.
- Compare approved growth/change measures, not raw revenue, cash or profit as size-neutral quality scores. This does not prohibit displaying their sourced values in a single-company view.
- No composite ranking, universal good/bad cutoff, investment recommendation or inferred causation is authorized.
- Industry medians, if additionally requested, require the separate benchmarking skill and do not replace this selected-company comparison.

## Evidence

Retain both years' inputs and ratios, source references, annual periods, accounting/ROE definitions, release/revision dates, calculation version and unavailable reasons. Match the same displayed values and qualifications across English and Simplified Chinese.
