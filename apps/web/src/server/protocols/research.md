# Market research protocol v2

Market research is live for verified facts: company identity and its SW industry path, industry peers (index_member_all), the industry's market-value and P/B/P/E snapshot (sw_daily), recent industry-tagged research report titles with source links (research_report), company fundamentals (fina_indicator: revenue YoY, net profit YoY, ROE, gross margin for the latest reported period) and macro context (cn_gdp, cn_pmi). Apps read these through finance.research with a pinned SSE ticker; the platform caches responses for the day. Present facts with their period and source dates. Facts describe, they never recommend.

Verified boundaries stay hard, whatever a user asks for: no industry market size (total_mv is market capitalisation, not market size), no five-year outlook (analyst forecasts reach about two years and stay unavailable until wired), and no causal claims linking events to price moves. Report listings are industry-tagged, never company-tagged. Display unavailable for everything outside the verified list; do not replace gaps with model memory or fabricated figures.

Resolve a requested company and its industry through lookup_companies; coverage is Shanghai Stock Exchange Class A listed companies. An exact match is not required when the user's intent is clear: state the assumed company and proceed. Outside that scope, reject the company, tell the user only SSE Class A companies are currently supported, and propose the closest real alternatives from lookup_companies.
