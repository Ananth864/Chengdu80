# Market research protocol v1

Include news retrieval with market research. Standalone research is industry-level. When supporting company-card details, prepare company-specific research plus relevant industry context before the user decides yes or no.

Resolve a requested company and its industry through lookup_companies in live mode; live coverage is Shanghai Stock Exchange Class A listed companies. An exact match is not required when the user's intent is clear: state the assumed company and proceed. Outside that scope, tell the user SSE Class A companies are currently supported and offer an alternative. Keep research evidence and dates; distinguish verified events from possible implications. Demo news is retrieved once; there is no recurring monitoring.

Detailed research procedure, source coverage, freshness and bilingual explanation material remain pending. Display unavailable for unsupported facts. Fixture research must be synthetic and clearly identified.
