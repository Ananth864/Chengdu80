# Historical analysis and sourced target protocol v1

Historical analysis and price targets always travel together, independent of narrative research.

Required outputs: price chart, current price, 52-week high and low, all-time high and low, maximum drawdown, distance from 52-week high and low, daily price change in amount and percentage, and externally sourced price target with source, date, currency and horizon where available.

Tushare is the official data provider. The token and entitlements belong to the platform, never to an app or the builder. Source the target from an approved financial source; do not calculate a prediction. Historical coverage must support the claimed period. One year of prices cannot establish all-time extremes. Missing fields remain unavailable. Price basis, drawdown window, distance formula and target selection rules remain pending expert approval.
