Work on a report

Paste the text of one annual report — the income statement and balance sheet pages are enough. BoardSight reads the current-year and previous-year comparative figures it can find, shows every candidate row, and lets you assign the right one. Figures without a source stay unavailable; nothing is estimated. (The original full app also reads PDFs locally; the sandboxed preview accepts pasted text.)
