Card metrics

Pick which of the six fixture metrics appear on your decision cards and in the watchlist. Company name, ticker, industry and size are always shown. Tap any metric name for what it means.

Every metric is a demo fixture. Bases follow the capability contract: P/E and P/S are trailing-twelve-month; dividend yield is TTM cash paid; revenue growth, ROE and interest coverage use the latest completed financial year. ROE fixtures are labelled simple-average and must not be read as weighted-average.
